# Page templates: the holistic look

A full inventory of every page the site serves, which template each one uses,
and a proposal for getting from the three-and-a-half templates we have to two.
Written to answer two standing questions: "have we done this page?" (section 1
is the answer, bookmark it) and "why does the bottom of a city page change
color with the time of day while a guide never does?" (section 3).

This is a hand-written doc. It is not generated and CI does not regenerate it,
unlike `docs/city-pages-review.md`.

---

## 2. The templates we actually have

Counted by "what would you have to edit to change every page's shell," there
are three and a half:

- **Template A, the instrument.** Live SkyBackdrop, full React app, the cream
  `.seo-sheet` below the stage. Used by `/` and the 25 city pages. But `/` is a
  hand-written copy: its FAQ, footer and head mirror the generator's by hand,
  with keep-in-sync comments. That is the half-template.
- **Template B, editorial.** Static masthead sky, no React, `seo.css`. Hub,
  corridors, about, 404. Head built by `editorialHead()` in
  `gen-location-pages.mjs`.
- **Template C, guides.** Static masthead sky, no React, `article.css`. Guides
  hub and articles. Head built by `articleHead()` / `articleHeadForHub()` in
  `gen-articles.mjs`.

B and C are the same idea (a reading page: masthead sky, cream sheet, fixed
ink) implemented twice. The shared furniture already lives in
`scripts/lib/page.mjs` (footer links, disclaimer, analytics, breadcrumbs,
masthead markup), which is why the drift so far is small. But it is real:

- **The footer renders differently on B and C.** `seo.css` styles it
  left-aligned, sentence case, with a top border. `article.css` restyles the
  same markup centered, uppercase mono, no border. Same links, two looks;
  a reader going from `/about/` to a guide watches the footer change costume.
- **The FAQ block is styled twice** (`seo.css` and `article.css`), at slightly
  different sizes, for identical markup.
- **The page header is two components**: `.map-intro` (B and the city pages)
  and `.guide__header` (C), with near-identical but separately tuned type
  ramps.
- **The head is built in three places**: `page()`, `editorialHead()`, and the
  two article head builders. The city head carries mobile-web-app metas the
  others lack; each new head decision has to be made three times.

## 3. The time-of-day inconsistency, diagnosed

What Kelly is seeing on the location pages is not the sheet changing color.
The sheet is already fixed cream everywhere (`.seo-sheet` and `.guide__sheet`
are the same surface, deliberately, with fixed contrast ratios). What changes
is everything around it.

A city page boots the full app, and SkyBackdrop paints the entire viewport,
fixed, with the live sky for that hour: light blue at noon, amber at dusk,
near-black at night. The heading above the sheet, the margins around it, and
the footer below it all ride that live sky. A guide instead ships the static
masthead: the resting clear-midday gradient, the soft sun, and the feathered
ridge, always. So the same reading material sits in two different rooms, and
which room you get depends on the clock.

On the top half of a city page that is the product. The stage is an
instrument, the sky is the reading, and a dark sky at night on a page about
"how is the air right now" is honest. The problem is confined to the bottom
half, where reference material (landmarks, provenance, FAQ) sits on an
instrument it has nothing to do with, and where the guides set a different
expectation for what a Smokeshow reading surface looks like.

## 4. Proposal: two templates

**Template 1: Live.** Has coordinates, boots the app, live sky. `/` and the 25
city pages. **Template 2: Reading.** No coordinates, no React, masthead sky,
cream sheet. Hub, corridors, about, 404, guides hub, articles. No third
template; the corridor/hub/about pages are reading pages that happen to be
generated from data instead of markdown.

Status: moves 1 through 3 are implemented on this branch. Move 4 remains open.

Getting there is four moves, ordered by value:

1. **Give the city pages' bottom half the ridge.** Render the masthead ridge
   (the two feathered hill paths, not the gradient or sun) behind the
   `.seo-sheet` on city pages, in the same fixed ink-on-transparency the
   masthead uses. The hills read against any sky state, so the reading
   section carries the same landscape identity as the guides at every hour
   while the live sky above stays the instrument. This is the "gradient flows
   down, hills in the background" instinct, scoped to what stays honest: the
   sky keeps telling the truth about now, and the hills are what make it the
   same site. The alternative considered and rejected: crossfading the live
   sky into the resting midday gradient below the stage. That puts two skies
   on one page, and a midday backdrop behind a page whose top half says it is
   night is exactly the kind of asserted condition these pages are built to
   avoid.
2. **Merge B and C's furniture.** One footer ruleset, one FAQ ruleset, one
   header component instead of `.map-intro` vs `.guide__header`. Mechanically:
   move the shared blocks into one stylesheet both entries import (or collapse
   `editorial.js` and `article.js` into one entry that imports `seo.css` +
   `article.css` remainders). Pick one footer look; the centered mono one from
   the guides is the better-considered of the two.
3. **Merge the head builders.** One `pageHead()` in `scripts/lib/page.mjs`
   taking `{ title, description, url, ogType, article? }`, used by both
   generators. The og:type and article-time metas are parameters, not reasons
   for three functions.
4. **Fold `/` into the generator, eventually.** The homepage's hand-mirrored
   FAQ and footer are the last keep-in-sync comments in the system. Lower
   urgency because tests guard the copy rules, but it is the remaining
   half-template.

Also worth a decision, not part of the merge: `/asdfasdf/` (the front-end
review candidate) still pins `#map-slot` rules in `seo.css`, and
`/public/ifhghs/demo/` is a third stray. If their reviews are done, deleting
both removes the last pages that answer to no template.

## 5. What deliberately stays as it is

- The cream sheet not flipping with the air. That is a decision with measured
  contrast ratios behind it, restated in `seo.css` and `article.css`, and it
  is what already makes the two halves of the site feel related.
- The static payload rules from CLAUDE.md: nothing here adds a condition,
  a level name, or a number to any static page.
- The two-generator split (data-driven pages vs markdown articles). Two
  generators is fine; two shells is the problem, and after the merge both
  generators assemble the same shell from `scripts/lib/page.mjs`.
